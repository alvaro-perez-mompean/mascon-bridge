- **The virtual device is called `mascon-bridge` now.** It used to announce itself as
  `One Handle MasCon for Nintendo Switch`, with ZUIKI as the manufacturer. Neither the

  screen still shows the old name; that comes from Steam's own controller database
  rather than from the device, and it is not a sign of anything being wrong.
- **Both READMEs gained a trademarks and disclaimer section**: an unofficial tool
  written by an individual, with no affiliation to ZUIKI Inc., East Japan Railway
  Company or Nintendo Co., Ltd.; their trademarks named only to identify the hardware
  and software this works with; and why the vendor and product ids match the real
  device.
- Cleared one remaining test warning (xUnit1026), which the v0.2.0 release build
  carried.

Nothing changed in how the bridge behaves: the notch values, the HID report and the
